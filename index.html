Privacy & Safety

UniFind Campus uses Supabase to share public-safe report details across devices. Contact details are hidden by default and are included in public listings only when the reporter explicitly opts in. Item photos are publicly viewable; never upload sensitive documents or personal information.


Guidelines

  • Only report items you personally lost or found on campus.
  • Do not include sensitive personal information in public descriptions.
  • Always verify ownership before returning an item.
  • Meet in public, well-lit campus locations when exchanging items.
  • The footer control does not delete shared reports. Contact an approved moderator for an authorized data request.
MODERATOR ACCESS

Moderator sign in

Only an account approved in Supabase can mark reports as resolved. Public accounts cannot gain moderator access through this form.

Administrator setup is explained in README.md. Do not create a public sign-up flow for moderator access.
